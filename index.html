About the company

Before Stellr IT, Andrew spent his 23 year career inside some of the most demanding environments there are: a top law firm whose clients included a prime minister, a Silicon Valley startup led by a Navy SEAL turned founder, and some of the most prestigious private schools around. Across all of them, he saw the same pattern: non technical executives making technical decisions, without the guidance or staffing to set up or maintain what they'd bought, often sold on solutions that took far more upkeep than anyone expected.

Stellr IT exists for the businesses in between. The ones that don't need, or want, a full time IT hire, but aren't technical enough to run their own systems properly. Andrew is the person doing the work behind every engagement: user and device management, SOC 2 audit support, security monitoring, and Privacy Officer duties for clients who need them. Based in Canada, working with companies around the world.

When he's not armpits deep in the technical weeds, you'll find Andrew hanging out with his family and golden retriever, golfing (he's a 3 handicap if you need a ringer for your scramble team), fishing, making espresso, or following the F1 Mercedes team.
